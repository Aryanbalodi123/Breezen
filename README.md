# 🌬️ Breezen – Breathe. Pause. Flow.

**Breezen** is a modern, minimal meditation app designed to help you breathe deeper, slow down, and reconnect with your inner stillness.

> _Breezen_ blends the calm of a breeze with the clarity of zen.

---

## 🧘 What is Breezen?

Breezen offers guided meditations, breathing exercises, and ambient soundscapes to help users:
- Reduce stress and anxiety
- Improve focus and mindfulness
- Build a daily meditation habit

Whether you're a beginner or an experienced meditator, Breezen provides a serene space to pause, breathe, and grow mentally stronger.
